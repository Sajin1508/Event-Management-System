About the Platform

Campus events, managed end to end

Event Management System brings student registrations, event operations, and attendance into one shared campus workspace.

01

Discover & reserve

Browse technical, cultural, arts, and science events. Reserve individual or team passes and keep your QR ticket in one place.

02

Coordinate events

Faculty and event teams can publish schedules, set capacity and fees, add payment QR codes, and manage registrations.

03

Check in & certify

Scan passes at the gate, update attendance, and make participation certificates available to checked-in students.
